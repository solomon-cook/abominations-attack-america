# Known limitations

- New interactive MVP matches use the complete 336-cell human-audited flat-top honeycomb board. Board data and project-owner same-scale visual review have user-authorized sign-off; independent review, audited-board online persistence verification, and overall release sign-off remain open. The nine-space graph remains an isolated development fixture.
- Development compatibility locations are rendered as compact flat-top hex pins with cream borders; their geometry is a UI fixture treatment, not evidence of the physical board's cell size, silhouette, or authored content.
- The legacy candidate shell and development compatibility geometry are not physical board definitions; separate source-gated rules boundaries remain unresolved.
- Monster Challenge giant-unit ordering and America-saved victory are implemented in the development ruleset; giant card placement asks the owner to choose any owned branch base, including one already stomped, based on the card text and rulebook page 14. Deploy-step placement into a Monster space does not itself start an immediate battle, based on rulebook pages 7 and 14. Some card stacking, visibility, and permanent-lifecycle rulings remain source-gated; Blonde Lure, all 16 Research effects, and the sourced giant/X-Fighters placement and combat lifecycle are implemented in the development ruleset.
- The local browser playtest is not evidence of a deployed online service. Live Postgres, staging, production, backup/restore, and external alerting are not configured.
- Manual keyboard-only, screen-reader, contrast, touch, and browser-matrix reviews remain open even though automated source contracts and responsive checks exist.
- Audio is intentionally absent; all current required outcomes are presented as text.
- Reference photographs and generated study assets require final content/IP approval before public distribution.
