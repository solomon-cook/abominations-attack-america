# CIV-Inspired UI/UX Redesign Plan

This checklist tracks the approved board-first UI changes. Check an item only after its behavior is implemented and verified in the rendered desktop and mobile flows where applicable. Suggestion **#10 is intentionally deferred** and must stay out of this work.

## Approved changes

- [x] **1. Compact player medallion and quick access** — Show the active monster portrait in a circular health ring with numeric health and infamy. Keep military and card records one tap away. Verify health updates and record access at desktop and phone sizes.
  - Progress: The portrait medallion, health ring, health number, and infamy are present. Browser checks at 1280×720, 390×844, and 320×740 confirm the Monster record shows numeric Health and Infamy with a health-gradient ring, and Monster/Military records open from their tabs.
- [x] **2. Slim top HUD and menu** — Keep active player, round, phase, and essential counters in a compact header. Place secondary actions in the game menu. Verify the menu opens, closes, and supports keyboard use.
  - Progress: Compact header styling and a Menu disclosure are in place. Keyboard opening, Tab traversal through menu items, Settings opening, and Escape closing passed at 1280×720. At 390×844 the menu fits within the viewport, Settings uses a scrollable panel without document overflow, and Escape closes it.
- [x] **3. One selected-piece panel** — Put the selected piece’s portrait, concise stats, relevant ability, and contextual actions in one place. Avoid repeated health, infamy, and stats elsewhere in the HUD.
  - Progress: The selected monster/unit projection test confirms concise stats and the relevant ability are grouped in one tray; ordinary military details do not repeat monster Health or Infamy. The mobile sheet suppresses the duplicate monster record and keeps the one-tap player record available.
- [x] **4. Clear primary action** — Give the stable primary control phase-appropriate labels and behavior: Confirm move, Next piece, Continue to Fight, Hold, or End movement. Verify every movement state and avoid double-submitting an action.
  - Progress: Browser checks show the initial Hold action, select a legal route, confirm it, and advance to the available next action (Continue to Fight in the audited local match). The action changes after submission rather than resubmitting the same route; the queue keeps End movement available for outstanding orders.
- [x] **5. Portrait movement queue** — Show the monster and eligible units as a compact portrait row with remaining-order count, selected state, and completion markers. Selecting a portrait selects and focuses that piece; provide an expanded accessible list when needed.
  - Progress: The desktop queue sizes to its actual contents: the one-piece queue occupies about 132px instead of reserving 520px, with a 520px cap for larger rosters. Queue projection checks cover a selected movable unit, held and completed markers, remaining-order count, and a four-piece roster.
- [x] **6. Board-aware panels and exclusive inspectors** — Keep selected pieces visible around the HUD and avoid overlapping major sheets, dialogs, and inspectors. Verify focus, camera placement, close behavior, and keyboard navigation.
  - Progress: Zoom/reset controls sit in the top HUD clear of the portrait rail. The minimap stays hidden unless Map is open and hides when the record closes. Desktop and phone browser bounds checks verify it clears the movement queue, command sheet, controls, and portrait rail. It compacts to 144×66px at 320×740; the mobile sheet shrinks to fit its content up to a scrollable maximum. The deployment flow closes the phase side panel and offsets camera focus above the fixed phone action tray. The Military inspector stays inside desktop/mobile viewports and closes with Escape.
- [x] **7. Compact deployment tray** — Show unit portraits, reserve/deployed counts, concise stats, legal placement, and the Research alternative. Keep full military sheets available on demand. Verify deployment and redeployment.
  - Progress: The drawer shows unit records, reserve counts, legal deployment/redeployment choices, and the Research action. Browser checks place a legal unit on the 336-cell audited board at 320×740; desktop and 390×844 flows draw Research instead. Projection checks cover branch-to-Guard routing, redeployment choices, Guard Commander ownership, and giant records.
- [x] **8. Lighter routine encounters** — Use a smaller board-side encounter treatment, preserve meaningful choices and card inspection, keep major battles and challenges cinematic, and let players reveal remaining routine rolls at once.
  - Progress: Routine encounters use a centered compact dialog while the board remains visible. Browser flows reveal remaining rolls and inspect any card/reward offered; projection checks cover no-choice results, explicit Iron Stomach choices, and mutation card inspection. Challenge UI checks preserve its cinematic resolution stage.
- [x] **9. Player portrait rail** — Show player portraits, ownership, and active-turn state; selecting a portrait reveals public health, infamy, and position. Verify local, online, and spectator behavior.
  - Progress: The portrait rail and selectable public-status cards are implemented. The audit found that the rail disabled pointer events on its parent and the buttons inherited that setting; each portrait and public status card now accepts pointer input. Local click and Enter-key activation, plus online-player and spectator portrait access, expose public health, infamy, and position.
- [x] **11. Setup progression and tighter choices** — Show Monster → Branch → Lair → Starting choice progress, keep monster selection scannable, and reduce setup-panel board coverage. Verify every setup phase and phone layout.
  - Progress: Setup progress appears on the audited-board monster-selection screen. Monster cards are now equal-height and compact; phone panels occupy about 380px at 390×844 and keep the board visible. Branch cards scroll horizontally on phones and fit in a shorter desktop tray; lair selection remains a compact bottom prompt; starting-choice actions fit in a 189px tray at 390×844 and 205px at 320×740. Confirmed all four phases at 1280×720 and 390×844, plus 320px phone rendering with no document overflow.
- [x] **12. CIV-inspired visual system** — Apply the blue-green, brass, and ivory palette, portrait framing, consistent icon treatment, and readable display/body typography across the redesigned HUD.
  - Progress: Shared HUD tokens and styling were reviewed across setup/gameplay, with contrast and reduced-motion checks passing. Framed portraits, gold primary actions, consistent icon treatment, and readable typography are present across the flows.
- [x] **13. One expandable mobile command sheet** — On phones, combine the at-rest portrait, selected piece, orders, and relevant actions into one expandable sheet with generous touch targets. Verify safe areas, scrolling, and board access.
  - Progress: The player record, tabs, movement queue, selected-piece context, and action share one sheet. Browser checks at 320×740 and 390×844 verify 44px tabs, no page overflow, an on-screen Military inspector, internal scrolling for the long 320px roster, and legal board access. Mobile command and record anchors include safe-area insets.

## Deferred

- [ ] **10. Map label and noise changes — DEFERRED** — Do not change zoom-dependent labels/details, map-noise reduction, stack badges, grid lines, or movement-highlight styling as part of this plan.

- Setup flow was rechecked with screenshots at 320×740, 390×844, and 1280×720. Monster, branch, lair, and starting-choice stages render without document overflow; desktop branch/monster panels now fit their compact card content without clipping. The full audited-board playthrough passed again at 390×844 after these changes.

- Routine encounter results were checked at 1280×720 and 390×844: the dialog measured 820×439 and 374×313 pixels respectively, with no internal horizontal or vertical overflow.
