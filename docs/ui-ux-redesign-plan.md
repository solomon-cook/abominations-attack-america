# CIV-Inspired UI/UX Redesign Plan

This checklist tracks the approved board-first UI changes. Check an item only after its behavior is implemented and verified in the rendered desktop and mobile flows where applicable. Suggestion **#10 is intentionally deferred** and must stay out of this work.

## Approved changes

- [ ] **1. Compact player medallion and quick access** — Show the active monster portrait in a circular health ring with numeric health and infamy. Keep military and card records one tap away. Verify health updates and record access at desktop and phone sizes.
  - Progress: The portrait medallion, health ring, health number, and infamy are present. Expanding it reveals Monster, Military, and Map tabs. Mobile layout and access behavior remain to verify.
- [x] **2. Slim top HUD and menu** — Keep active player, round, phase, and essential counters in a compact header. Place secondary actions in the game menu. Verify the menu opens, closes, and supports keyboard use.
  - Progress: Compact header styling and a Menu disclosure are in place. Keyboard opening, Tab traversal through menu items, Settings opening, and Escape closing passed at 1280×720. At 390×844 the menu fits within the viewport, Settings uses a scrollable panel without document overflow, and Escape closes it.
- [ ] **3. One selected-piece panel** — Put the selected piece’s portrait, concise stats, relevant ability, and contextual actions in one place. Avoid repeated health, infamy, and stats elsewhere in the HUD.
  - Progress: Selected-piece stats have been reduced, and the persistent record is minimized by default. Audit each phase for duplicate information and verify the expanded panel.
- [ ] **4. Clear primary action** — Give the stable primary control phase-appropriate labels and behavior: Confirm move, Next piece, Continue to Fight, Hold, or End movement. Verify every movement state and avoid double-submitting an action.
  - Progress: Movement labels and next-piece selection logic are implemented. Verify after the monster moves, with movable and immovable military pieces, and after all orders resolve.
- [ ] **5. Portrait movement queue** — Show the monster and eligible units as a compact portrait row with remaining-order count, selected state, and completion markers. Selecting a portrait selects and focuses that piece; provide an expanded accessible list when needed.
  - Progress: Portrait buttons and counts are implemented and now sit beside the persistent command station. The desktop queue now sizes to its actual contents: the one-piece queue occupies about 132px instead of reserving 520px, while keeping a 520px cap for larger rosters. Selecting a portrait, completion markers, and larger-roster behavior remain to verify.
- [ ] **6. Board-aware panels and exclusive inspectors** — Keep selected pieces visible around the HUD and avoid overlapping major sheets, dialogs, and inspectors. Verify focus, camera placement, close behavior, and keyboard navigation.
  - Progress: Camera focus is offset around the HUD. Zoom/reset controls sit together in the top HUD, away from the board. A rendered review found the desktop group stranded in the center of the bar; it now sits beside the menu at 1024px and wider. Phone controls remain at x=12 in the left HUD row so they avoid the portrait rail. The overview minimap now stays hidden until the Map tab is open, and hides again when the record sheet closes. When opened, it sits above the desktop movement queue or directly under the phone HUD, clearing the command sheet. The open minimap at 390×844 measures 354×133px and ends 5px above the command sheet. Camera/minimap bounds passed audited-board checks; other inspector overlap and keyboard states remain.
- [ ] **7. Compact deployment tray** — Show unit portraits, reserve/deployed counts, concise stats, legal placement, and the Research alternative. Keep full military sheets available on demand. Verify deployment and redeployment.
  - Progress: The military drawer holds deployment details and research controls. In a live deployment, the full-width “Draw a research card” choice was visible and usable at 1280×720 and 390×844; drawing it revealed the card and advanced the turn. Unit placement, redeployment, and disabled-state review remain.
- [ ] **8. Lighter routine encounters** — Use a smaller board-side encounter treatment, preserve meaningful choices and card inspection, keep major battles and challenges cinematic, and let players reveal remaining routine rolls at once.
  - Progress: Routine encounters now use a centered, compact dialog instead of the full-screen stage; the map remains visible around it. “Reveal remaining rolls” remains available. Desktop and phone no-choice results fit without overflow; choice/card variants and full-screen battle/challenge styling still need review.
- [x] **9. Player portrait rail** — Show player portraits, ownership, and active-turn state; selecting a portrait reveals public health, infamy, and position. Verify local, online, and spectator behavior.
  - Progress: The portrait rail and selectable public-status cards are implemented. The audit found that the rail disabled pointer events on its parent and the buttons inherited that setting; each portrait and public status card now accepts pointer input. Local click and Enter-key activation, plus online-player and spectator portrait access, expose public health, infamy, and position.
- [x] **11. Setup progression and tighter choices** — Show Monster → Branch → Lair → Starting choice progress, keep monster selection scannable, and reduce setup-panel board coverage. Verify every setup phase and phone layout.
  - Progress: Setup progress appears on the audited-board monster-selection screen. Monster cards are now equal-height and compact; phone panels occupy about 380px at 390×844 and keep the board visible. Branch cards scroll horizontally on phones and fit in a shorter desktop tray; lair selection remains a compact bottom prompt; starting-choice actions fit in a 189px tray at 390×844 and 205px at 320×740. Confirmed all four phases at 1280×720 and 390×844, plus 320px phone rendering with no document overflow.
- [ ] **12. CIV-inspired visual system** — Apply the blue-green, brass, and ivory palette, portrait framing, consistent icon treatment, and readable display/body typography across the redesigned HUD.
  - Progress: Shared HUD tokens and styling are in place. Review every phase and accessibility contrast at supported viewport sizes.
- [ ] **13. One expandable mobile command sheet** — On phones, combine the at-rest portrait, selected piece, orders, and relevant actions into one expandable sheet with generous touch targets. Verify safe areas, scrolling, and board access.
  - Progress: The active-player record, tabs, movement queue, selected-piece context, and action now share one expandable sheet. At 320×740 and 390×844, opening it, reading the order list, and switching to Military were verified; safe-area and long-content scrolling checks remain.

## Deferred

- [ ] **10. Map label and noise changes — DEFERRED** — Do not change zoom-dependent labels/details, map-noise reduction, stack badges, grid lines, or movement-highlight styling as part of this plan.

- `npm test` passed for both the game-engine and API suites; `npm run typecheck` passed for web and API; `npm run build` passed.
- Accessibility, WCAG contrast, reduced-motion, responsive-layout, board-camera, and `git diff --check` verifications passed.
- `browser:audited:verify` passed at 1280×720 and 390×844; manual HUD menu keyboard checks passed at 1280×720. These do not replace the remaining phase-specific and assistive-technology review.
- `browser:audited:verify` passed at 1280×720 and 390×844, completing setup on the 336-cell audited board, camera/minimap interactions where available, movement, encounter resolution, and the Military Research draw alternative. Both viewports reported no runtime errors or missing terrain images.
- Map controls were visually checked at 320×740 and 390×844; all three buttons stay together in the HUD row and avoid the player portrait rail. Keyboard testing confirmed the game menu opens, focus advances through its actions, Settings opens, and Escape closes it.
- The map-control group was visually checked at desktop widths: it had been isolated near the center of the top bar, so it now aligns immediately before Menu at 1024px and wider. At 768px and narrower the existing left-aligned HUD placement is retained; 320px DOM bounds are x=12–148 with all three 44px targets visible.
- `browser:online:verify` passed through room creation, setup, reconnect recovery, spectator synchronization, a confirmed online move, and an Encounter that ended directly in Victory. Both an online player and spectator opened public status cards with health, Infamy, and position. The scenario conditionally skips Deploy and concession when the Encounter itself produces Victory.
- All 12 card-specific UI projection verifiers passed. A full test run also caught and fixed an online Atomic Breath allowance omission; the multi-target round now requests both first-round attacks before advancing.
- The minimap was visible after the Map tab had been selected and the record collapsed; that persisted view collided with the order queue. It now stays hidden when the record is closed. At 1280×720, opening Map places the overview at x=380, y=316, clear of the movement queue; at 390×844, opening Map places it at x=18, y=140 just above the expanded command sheet. Collapsing the sheet hides it again. The audited-browser verification now tests minimap interaction while open and hidden state after closing, and passes at both 1280×720 and 390×844.
- Setup flow was rechecked with screenshots at 320×740, 390×844, and 1280×720. Monster, branch, lair, and starting-choice stages render without document overflow; desktop branch/monster panels now fit their compact card content without clipping. The full audited-board playthrough passed again at 390×844 after these changes.
- Narrow-layout menu review passed at 390×844: keyboard opens Menu, Settings opens and scrolls inside the viewport, Escape closes it, and the document has no overflow.
- Routine encounter results were checked at 1280×720 and 390×844: the dialog measured 820×439 and 374×313 pixels respectively, with no internal horizontal or vertical overflow.
- Remaining work: verify every movement/order state, exclusive-inspector behavior, setup choices, deployment/redeployment, encounter choice/card variants, and mobile safe areas and long-content scrolling, then repeat final checks. Suggestion #10 remains deferred.
