## Resolution status

These rows separate development behavior from source verification and release approval. A row can close only after its rule-bearing facts are checked against the user's physical edition, evidence and ambiguity are recorded, the corresponding data and fixtures agree, and the required human sign-off is complete. A hosted scan, player aid, passing validator, or internal `verified` field cannot by itself close a row. No engine fallback may silently substitute an unverified value.
