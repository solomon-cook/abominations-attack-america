# Checked-in rulebook page alignment audit

## Scope and authority

This is a comparison with the 16 tracked English rulebook page images in [`tmp/pdfs/rulebook-pages/`](../tmp/pdfs/rulebook-pages/), not proof that those images are from the user's exact physical printing. The printed title and 2005 credits are visible in [page 1](../tmp/pdfs/rulebook-pages/page-01.jpg) and [page 16](../tmp/pdfs/rulebook-pages/page-16.jpg); no source PDF, extraction text, image-set manifest, or confirmed match to the user's copy is available here. The rulebook images are legible enough to audit their general rules against the current paraphrase and code. Physical-board, component, and card parity remains a separate review.

“Aligned” below means that the cited code/tests represent the cited rule at the checked-in image level. It does not certify underlying printed board coordinates, component transcriptions, card exceptions, or edition identity. A verifier passing is not evidence of substantive source alignment by itself: [`scripts/verify-rules-traceability.mjs`](../scripts/verify-rules-traceability.mjs) checks rules-reference heading coverage and matrix shape, not page text or engine behavior.

## Concrete discrepancies in the checked-in implementation

These are discrepancies relative to the cited images, independently checked against the current implementation. They are confirmed only at the checked-in-image level; no rule code, source status, board data, or rule text should change until the exact user edition and any intentional digital adaptation are recorded.

| ID | Source rule | Current implementation evidence | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-1: starting sequence | Page 5 says the first player places their monster at a lair, then deploys or draws Research; the player to the left repeats that combined sequence. | [`SetupState`](../packages/game-engine/src/setup.ts) has separate `lair-selection` and `starting-choice` phases; `chooseLair` accepts any seat and all seats complete lairs before `chooseStartingChoice` begins. [`setup.test.ts`](../packages/game-engine/src/setup.test.ts) exercises that phase order. | R-3: monster retreat | Page 9 requires a monster that attacked units and was forced to retreat to return to the adjacent space it came from; retreat may not cross a prohibited water barrier; if no legal retreat space exists, the monster must disappear. ## Independent second pass

A second read-only reviewer checked all 16 rendered page images against the engine, API, and UI. It confirmed R-1 through R-4 at the checked-in scan level, confirmed that Hollywood is an off-board zero-Health recovery state rather than a movement destination, identified both disappeared-monster and Hollywood destination-choice mismatches captured in R-5, and identified the reward-actor mismatch recorded in R-6. This review does not establish that the scanned 2005 image set matches the user's physical printing.
