## Initial missing-component inventory

Before this collection, the workspace had a rulebook-derived text reference and 16 rendered rulebook pages, but no standalone component/card image library. The physical game inventory therefore missing as component references was:

- 6 giant monster pieces: Gargantis, Konk, Megaclaw, Tomanagi, Toxicor, and Zorb.
- 44 military pieces:
  - 40 regular branch units: 8 each for Air Force, Army, Marines, Navy, and National Guard.
  - 2 black X-Fighters.
  - 2 giant military units: Mecha-Monster and Captain Colossal.
- 16 Monster Mutation cards.
- 16 Military Research cards.
- 15 plastic sliders.
- 65 tokens: 42 Infamy tokens and 23 Stomp markers.
- 3 six-sided dice.
- 13 record/reference tiles: 6 monster, 5 military-branch, and 2 giant military-unit tiles.
- 1 game board.
- 1 rulebook (already represented locally by the rendered rulebook pages and the structured rules reference).

Counts are cross-checked against the [BoardGameGeek component list](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/17835/monsters-menace-america) and the contemporary [RPGnet component review](https://www.rpg.net/reviews/view-printable.phtml?reviewNumber=11252).

## Coverage achieved

- **Monster minimum achieved:** all 6 monster record tiles, each photographed with its matching piece.
- **Military branches achieved:** all 5 military record tiles.
- Both giant military-unit record tiles are covered.
- All 16 Military Research card faces are represented across three source photographs.
- All 16 Monster Mutation card faces are represented across four source photographs.
- The board, monster pieces, military pieces, giant units, Infamy tokens, Stomp markers, dice, and in-use sliders are represented.
- 23 downloaded JPEG files have 23 distinct SHA-256 hashes; no byte-identical files were retained.

The source deck photographs deliberately overlap on a few cards. The overlap is inside otherwise unique photographs and is necessary to cover all 16 unique card faces in each deck without inventing or reconstructing cards.

## Remaining reference gaps

No known component category is entirely absent after this pass, but these reference-quality gaps remain:

- No isolated, straight-on, high-resolution scan for each of the 32 deck cards; the complete card-face coverage is in photographed sheets.
- No isolated photograph of all 15 plastic sliders; several red sliders are clearly visible attached to record tiles in the full-game setup.
- No clean one-to-one catalog photograph of every individual military sculpt and colour; the group photo and record tiles cover the branches, inventories, and unit types.
- The clean board photo is only 753 x 623. The 2840 x 1752 setup photograph is sharper but contains pieces and cards around/on the board.
- The local rulebook pages remain the strongest rules reference, but the original English PDF itself is not stored in this reference folder.
